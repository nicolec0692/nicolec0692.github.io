Movie Webpage Project

Context

For this project, my partner and I were assigned to create a fictional movie webpage using HTML and CSS. We decided to create an action and comedy movie called "A Debt," which has a meaningful storyline about friendship, money, and helping someone in danger. Our goal was to create a webpage that looked like a real movie advertisement and made visitors interested in learning more about our movie. We wanted to make our website creative by using different colors, fonts, images, and designs that matched the theme of our story.

Content

For our project, my partner and I first created a plan to organize our ideas and divide the work between us. Our movie is about two friends, Tim and Jersey, who try to rescue their friend Nina after she gets kidnapped by a gang because her father owes money. After Nina suddenly disappears, Tim and Jersey decide to take matters into their own hands and investigate what happened.

While creating our webpage, we used different HTML and CSS features to make the website look more interesting. We added a money bag logo, images, different fonts, and a brown gradient background to connect with the theme of money and debt. We also used text shadows, colored borders, and different sections to organize the information. Another feature we included was highlighting specific words in different colors to make important parts of our sentences stand out. We also added a newsletter subscription section and a GIF to make the webpage more creative and interactive.

Reflection

One challenge I faced was learning how to highlight only one word in a sentence and change its color using HTML and CSS. When i asked my partner if she had any challenges she said she didn't with her part. At first, I was confused about how to select just one word without changing the entire sentence. I learned that I could use the span tag in HTML and create a class in CSS to style that specific word.

If I had more time, I would add more pictures, improve the design, and include more information.
